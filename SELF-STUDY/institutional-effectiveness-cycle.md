# Institutional Effectiveness Cycle

## Purpose

Institutional effectiveness is the continuous process of evaluating whether SydTek University fulfills its mission, academic standards, learning outcomes, and strategic goals.

---

## Institutional Effectiveness Areas

The institution should evaluate:

- academic quality
- learner outcomes
- portfolio quality
- faculty assessment consistency
- student services effectiveness
- governance effectiveness
- technology infrastructure
- public verification systems
- interdisciplinary learning performance
- institutional sustainability

---

## Recommended Annual Cycle

| Stage | Activity |
|---|---|
| 1 | Collect institutional data |
| 2 | Review outcomes and analytics |
| 3 | Identify strengths and gaps |
| 4 | Implement improvements |
| 5 | Document actions |
| 6 | Reassess outcomes |

---

## Suggested Evidence Sources

Evidence may include:

- assessment reports
- learner analytics
- portfolio review
- faculty feedback
- student surveys
- public scholarship metrics
- governance review
- completion data
- external review

---

## Effectiveness Principle

Institutions remain credible through measurable reflection, documented improvement, and continuous review.
