# Public Scholarship Framework

## Purpose

SydTek University recognizes public scholarship as a legitimate form of academic contribution within competency-based and interdisciplinary education models.

Public scholarship includes educational, scholarly, technical, institutional, or interdisciplinary contributions made in publicly reviewable environments.

---

## Public Scholarship Examples

Examples may include:

- educational YouTube content
- GitHub repositories
- public datasets
- research publications
- blockchain governance participation
- institutional frameworks
- digital archives
- interdisciplinary systems
- technical documentation
- portfolio-based research

---

## Review Requirements

Public activity alone does not automatically equal scholarship.

Academic review should evaluate:

- educational value
- originality
- rigor
- evidence quality
- outcome alignment
- contribution significance
- interdisciplinary impact

---

## Public Scholarship Metrics

Possible metrics may include:

- citations
- downloads
- engagement
- repository forks
- contribution history
- portfolio review
- interdisciplinary adoption
- educational participation

---

## Scholarship Principle

Public scholarship expands access to learning, evidence, and institutional contribution beyond traditional academic publication systems.
