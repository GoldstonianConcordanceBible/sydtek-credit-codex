# Outcomes and Validation Framework

## Purpose

The Outcomes and Validation Framework defines how SydTek University evaluates whether competency-based learning pathways produce measurable educational value.

---

## Validation Categories

Validation may include:

- learning outcome achievement
- portfolio quality
- faculty assessment consistency
- interdisciplinary competency growth
- public scholarship contribution
- learner persistence
- capstone performance
- graduate outcomes
- external review
- industry relevance

---

## Evidence Sources

Validation evidence may include:

- rubric performance
- portfolio review
- GitHub repositories
- blockchain verification
- public scholarship metrics
- learner analytics
- assessment data
- external evaluator review
- peer collaboration records
- competency mapping

---

## Longitudinal Evidence

The SydTek framework may evaluate long-term evidence such as:

- sustained learner participation
- repeated competency demonstration
- interdisciplinary progression
- public contribution growth
- portfolio expansion
- institutional engagement over time

---

## Educational Data Principle

Educational interaction data becomes institutionally valuable when:

- ethically collected
- transparently documented
- mapped to learning outcomes
- reviewed through assessment frameworks
- connected to measurable competency

---

## Validation Principle

The goal of validation is not popularity.

The goal is measurable educational effectiveness supported by evidence.
