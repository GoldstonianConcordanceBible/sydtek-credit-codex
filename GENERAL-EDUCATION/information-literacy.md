# Information Literacy Requirement

## Purpose

The Information Literacy requirement develops the ability to locate, evaluate, synthesize, cite, and apply information responsibly.

---

## Competency Areas

Learners should demonstrate:

- source evaluation
- citation practice
- research strategy
- misinformation awareness
- synthesis of evidence
- responsible use of AI tools
- documentation of claims

---

## Evidence Examples

- annotated bibliography
- research log
- source comparison
- citation exercise
- evidence map
- AI-use disclosure
- literature summary

---

## Information Literacy Principle

In an AI-native world, learners must know how to verify, cite, question, and responsibly use information.
