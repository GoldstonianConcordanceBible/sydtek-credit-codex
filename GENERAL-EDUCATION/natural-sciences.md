# Natural Sciences Requirement

## Purpose

The Natural Sciences requirement develops scientific literacy, evidence evaluation, observation, and empirical reasoning.

---

## Competency Areas

Learners should demonstrate:

- scientific method understanding
- evidence evaluation
- observation and measurement
- basic data interpretation
- hypothesis testing
- responsible use of scientific sources

---

## Evidence Examples

- lab reports
- science reflections
- data analysis
- research summaries
- environmental observations
- case study analysis

---

## Natural Sciences Principle

Scientific literacy helps learners distinguish evidence, inference, uncertainty, and responsible application.
