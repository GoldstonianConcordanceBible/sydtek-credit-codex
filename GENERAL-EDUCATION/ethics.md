# Ethics Requirement

## Purpose

The Ethics requirement develops moral reasoning, professional responsibility, social accountability, and decision-making judgment.

---

## Competency Areas

Learners should demonstrate:

- ethical analysis
- professional responsibility
- conflict evaluation
- stakeholder awareness
- integrity in decision-making
- responsible technology use
- accountability in public work

---

## Evidence Examples

- ethics case studies
- governance reflections
- professional conduct analysis
- technology ethics papers
- applied decision frameworks
- portfolio ethics statement

---

## Ethics Principle

Competency without ethics can become harm.

SydTek requires learners to connect skill with responsibility.
