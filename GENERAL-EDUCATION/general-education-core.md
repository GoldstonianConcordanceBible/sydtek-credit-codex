# General Education Core

## Purpose

The General Education Core ensures that associate-level and bachelor-level pathways include broad academic preparation beyond specialization.

General education supports:

- communication
- quantitative reasoning
- scientific literacy
- ethical reasoning
- humanities awareness
- social understanding
- information literacy
- interdisciplinary thinking

## General Education Principle

Specialized programs build depth.

General education builds breadth.

A credible undergraduate pathway needs both.
