# Humanities Requirement

## Purpose

The Humanities requirement develops interpretation, cultural literacy, historical awareness, and reflective judgment.

---

## Competency Areas

Learners should demonstrate:

- textual interpretation
- historical awareness
- cultural analysis
- philosophical reasoning
- aesthetic understanding
- ethical reflection
- comparative thinking

---

## Evidence Examples

- interpretive essays
- historical analysis
- philosophy reflections
- literature reviews
- cultural case studies
- comparative frameworks

---

## Humanities Principle

The humanities help learners understand meaning, culture, history, values, and the human condition.
