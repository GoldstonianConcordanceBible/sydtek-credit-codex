# Mathematics Requirement

## Purpose

The Mathematics requirement develops quantitative reasoning, numerical literacy, logic, and problem-solving.

---

## Competency Areas

Learners should demonstrate:

- arithmetic and algebraic reasoning
- quantitative interpretation
- data literacy
- financial or statistical reasoning
- logical problem-solving
- model-based thinking

---

## Evidence Examples

- problem sets
- data analysis
- financial models
- statistical summaries
- quantitative reflections
- applied calculations

---

## Mathematics Principle

Quantitative reasoning allows learners to evaluate claims, interpret data, and make disciplined decisions.
