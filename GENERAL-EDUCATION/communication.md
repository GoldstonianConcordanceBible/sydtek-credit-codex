# Communication Requirement

## Purpose

The Communication requirement develops clear written, oral, visual, and digital communication.

Learners should demonstrate the ability to explain ideas, organize arguments, document evidence, and communicate with appropriate audiences.

---

## Competency Areas

Learners should demonstrate:

- written clarity
- oral presentation
- digital communication
- evidence-based argument
- audience awareness
- revision and editing
- documentation standards

---

## Evidence Examples

- essays
- presentations
- video explanations
- technical documentation
- public reflections
- portfolio narratives
- research summaries

---

## Communication Principle

Competency must be communicated clearly to be reviewed, shared, transferred, and applied.
