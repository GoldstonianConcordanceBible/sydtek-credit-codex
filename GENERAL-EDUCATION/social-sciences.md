# Social Sciences Requirement

## Purpose

The Social Sciences requirement develops understanding of human behavior, institutions, communities, economics, governance, and society.

---

## Competency Areas

Learners should demonstrate:

- social analysis
- institutional reasoning
- behavioral understanding
- civic awareness
- economic literacy
- governance literacy
- evidence-based social interpretation

---

## Evidence Examples

- case studies
- policy analysis
- social research summaries
- governance reflections
- community analysis
- institutional maps

---

## Social Sciences Principle

Social science helps learners understand people, institutions, communities, systems, and decision-making.
