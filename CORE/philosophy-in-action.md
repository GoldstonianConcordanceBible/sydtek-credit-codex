# Philosophy in Action

Philosophy in Action is the institutional principle that learning must produce measurable demonstration.

Students are not evaluated only by:

- attendance
- passive observation
- memorization
- or hidden completion

Students are evaluated through visible application.

Application may include:

- building systems
- publishing work
- solving problems
- demonstrating competency
- participating in governance
- contributing to repositories
- completing interdisciplinary projects
- conducting research
- producing public artifacts

The SydTek framework treats knowledge as actionable.

Competency is not fully realized until it produces evidence.

This model supports:

- portfolio-based learning
- interdisciplinary education
- public scholarship
- decentralized science
- applied research
- competency verification

Philosophy in Action functions as the operational bridge between theory and measurable contribution.
