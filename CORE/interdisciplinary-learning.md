# Interdisciplinary Learning Framework

SydTek University supports interdisciplinary learning across multiple domains of knowledge.

Interdisciplinary learning recognizes that modern problems frequently require integrated competencies rather than isolated specialization.

Programs may combine:

- technology
- humanities
- business
- governance
- communication
- ethics
- systems thinking
- applied research
- media
- theology
- blockchain
- artificial intelligence
- decentralized science

The SydTek framework encourages:

- cross-domain competency
- systems thinking
- collaborative learning
- transferable skills
- portfolio integration
- applied interdisciplinary projects

Interdisciplinary pathways must still maintain:

- measurable learning outcomes
- assessment standards
- faculty oversight
- competency mapping
- academic integrity
- documented rigor

The purpose of interdisciplinary learning is not to reduce academic depth.

The purpose is to integrate multiple forms of expertise into coherent applied competency.
