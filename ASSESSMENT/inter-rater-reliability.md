# Inter-Rater Reliability Framework

## Purpose

Inter-rater reliability ensures consistency across faculty reviewers, assessors, and academic evaluators.

This framework supports fairness, quality control, and accreditation readiness.

---

## Why It Matters

Competency-based education depends on consistent judgment.

If two qualified reviewers evaluate the same artifact, their conclusions should be reasonably aligned.

---

## Reliability Practices

SydTek should use:

- shared rubrics
- reviewer training
- calibration sessions
- sample artifact scoring
- second-review protocols
- appeal procedures
- documentation audits

---

## Calibration Process

Recommended process:

1. Select sample artifacts.
2. Have multiple reviewers score independently.
3. Compare scoring differences.
4. Discuss interpretation of rubric criteria.
5. Revise rubric language if needed.
6. Repeat until scoring consistency improves.

---

## Second Review Triggers

A second review may be required when:

- student appeals a score
- artifact is borderline
- artifact is unusually complex
- faculty detects possible academic integrity issue
- artifact contributes to capstone, graduate, or doctoral review

---

## Documentation

Inter-rater reliability records should include:

- reviewer names
- artifact ID
- initial scores
- final consensus score
- scoring differences
- calibration notes
- rubric changes

---

## Reliability Principle

Public evidence increases transparency.

Reliable faculty assessment increases academic legitimacy.
