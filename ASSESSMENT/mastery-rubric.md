# Mastery Rubric

## Purpose

The Mastery Rubric defines how student competency is evaluated across micro-credentials, courses, certificates, and degree pathways.

Public evidence may show activity.

Assessment determines mastery.

---

## Mastery Levels

| Level | Status | Description |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | Not Submitted | No evidence submitted |
| 1 | Emerging | Evidence is incomplete or does not yet meet the stated outcome |
| 2 | Developing | Evidence partially meets the outcome but needs revision |
| 3 | Proficient | Evidence meets the stated outcome |
| 4 | Advanced | Evidence exceeds the stated outcome with strong analysis, originality, or application |
| 5 | Distinguished | Evidence demonstrates exceptional mastery, public contribution, or transferable model value |

---

## Minimum Completion Standard

For credit-bearing completion, learners must achieve at least:

**Level 3 — Proficient**

Faculty may require revision for any artifact below Level 3.

---

## Assessment Dimensions

Each artifact should be evaluated using the following dimensions:

1. Outcome Alignment  
2. Evidence Quality  
3. Applied Competency  
4. Reflection and Analysis  
5. Communication  
6. Academic or Professional Integrity  
7. Portfolio Readiness

---

## Mastery Rule

An artifact is not assessed by visibility alone.

A public artifact becomes academic evidence only when it demonstrates the stated learning outcome at the required mastery level.
