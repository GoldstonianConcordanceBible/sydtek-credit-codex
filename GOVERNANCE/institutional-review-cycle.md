# Institutional Review Cycle

## Purpose

The Institutional Review Cycle defines the process for periodic review of institutional effectiveness, academic quality, governance structures, and strategic direction.

Continuous review supports:

- accreditation readiness
- academic integrity
- institutional adaptability
- policy improvement
- long-term sustainability

---

## Recommended Review Areas

The institution should periodically review:

- mission alignment
- academic outcomes
- assessment quality
- faculty governance
- student services
- financial sustainability
- technology systems
- accessibility compliance
- portfolio effectiveness
- public verification systems

---

## Suggested Review Schedule

| Review Area | Recommended Frequency |
|---|---:|
| Academic outcomes | Annual |
| Policy review | Annual |
| Curriculum review | Every 2–3 years |
| Governance review | Every 2–3 years |
| Strategic planning | Every 3–5 years |
| Institutional mission review | Every 3–5 years |

---

## Documentation Requirements

Institutional review records should include:

- review date
- participating reviewers
- findings
- recommendations
- action items
- implementation timeline
- follow-up status

---

## Review Principle

Institutions remain credible through documented reflection, improvement, and accountability.
