# Board of Trustees Framework

## Purpose

The Board of Trustees provides institutional oversight, continuity, accountability, and long-term stewardship for SydTek University.

The Board exists to protect:

- institutional mission
- academic integrity
- financial sustainability
- governance continuity
- student interests
- public trust

---

## Recommended Board Structure

Recommended board composition:

- academic representatives
- industry or professional representatives
- governance advisors
- legal or compliance advisors
- financial oversight members
- interdisciplinary contributors

---

## Board Responsibilities

The Board may:

- approve strategic direction
- review institutional performance
- oversee financial sustainability
- approve major policies
- review accreditation readiness
- support institutional continuity
- oversee executive leadership accountability

---

## Board Documentation

Board records should include:

- meeting agendas
- attendance
- minutes
- resolutions
- policy approvals
- financial reviews
- institutional review summaries

---

## Board Principle

Strong institutions require continuity beyond any single founder, faculty member, or administrator.
