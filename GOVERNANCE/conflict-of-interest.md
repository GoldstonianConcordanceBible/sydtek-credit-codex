# Conflict of Interest Policy

## Purpose

This policy supports ethical institutional governance and protects academic decision-making from improper influence.

---

## Conflict Areas

Potential conflicts may include:

- financial interests
- token ownership influence
- hiring decisions
- grading relationships
- governance voting
- vendor relationships
- family relationships
- institutional partnerships

---

## Disclosure Requirement

Individuals serving in governance, faculty, assessment, or institutional leadership roles should disclose actual or potential conflicts of interest.

---

## Management of Conflicts

Possible actions may include:

- disclosure documentation
- recusal from voting
- independent review
- reassignment of evaluation
- oversight committee review

---

## Institutional Principle

Transparency strengthens trust, fairness, and institutional legitimacy.
