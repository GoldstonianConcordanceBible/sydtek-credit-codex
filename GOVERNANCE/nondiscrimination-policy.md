# Nondiscrimination Policy

## Purpose

SydTek University seeks to maintain a learning environment grounded in fairness, dignity, respect, and institutional integrity.

The institution does not discriminate in the administration of educational policies, admissions practices, academic review, advising, accessibility support, or student services except where lawful mission-specific requirements may apply within future faith-based implementation layers.

---

## Covered Areas

This policy applies to:

- admissions
- advising
- assessment
- student services
- academic participation
- grievance review
- faculty interaction
- institutional processes

---

## Accessibility and Equity

SydTek supports reasonable accessibility accommodations and equitable access to academic participation within the limits of institutional capacity and documented policy.

---

## Institutional Neutrality Layer

The SydTek infrastructure layer is intentionally faith-neutral and adaptable.

Future institutional adapters may define additional mission-specific expectations consistent with applicable law and institutional identity.

---

## Nondiscrimination Principle

A credible institution requires fair, consistent, and transparent treatment of learners and contributors.
