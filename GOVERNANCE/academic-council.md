# Academic Council Framework

## Purpose

The Academic Council serves as the primary academic oversight body within the SydTek competency-based education framework.

The Academic Council protects:

- academic quality
- curriculum standards
- assessment integrity
- faculty review consistency
- institutional effectiveness

---

## Recommended Membership

The Academic Council may include:

- Faculty-of-Record representatives
- curriculum specialists
- assessment reviewers
- interdisciplinary contributors
- registrar representation
- institutional leadership

---

## Academic Council Responsibilities

The Council may:

- approve academic programs
- review learning outcomes
- approve curriculum revisions
- oversee assessment frameworks
- review faculty qualifications
- monitor institutional effectiveness
- support accreditation readiness
- review appeals and exceptions

---

## Meeting Requirements

The Academic Council should meet on a regular documented schedule.

Recommended documentation includes:

- agendas
- attendance
- motions
- decisions
- review summaries
- policy revisions

---

## Council Principle

Competency-based education requires documented academic oversight and continuous review.
