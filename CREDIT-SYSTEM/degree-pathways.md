# Degree Pathways Framework

## Purpose

This file defines the academic pathway structure for SydTek’s competency-based framework.

These pathways are internal academic architecture models and do not claim accreditation or degree-granting authority unless recognized by an appropriate governing or accrediting body.

---

## Foundational Certificate

Requirements:

- 12 credits
- 4 courses
- 48 micro-credentials

Purpose:

- introduce foundational competency
- establish portfolio habits
- document early evidence
- support beginner-level academic or workforce development

---

## Professional Certificate

Requirements:

- 30 credits
- 10 courses
- 120 micro-credentials

Purpose:

- demonstrate focused competency
- build applied portfolio evidence
- support professional development
- prepare for advanced pathway entry

---

## Associate-Level Pathway

Requirements:

- 60 credits
- 20 courses
- 240 micro-credentials

Purpose:

- build foundational academic breadth
- establish general education competency
- develop interdisciplinary fluency
- produce public evidence of applied learning

---

## Bachelor-Level Pathway

Requirements:

- 120 credits
- 40 courses
- 480 micro-credentials

Purpose:

- demonstrate structured academic mastery
- complete general education
- complete concentration requirements
- develop an advanced public portfolio
- complete capstone-level applied work

---

## Master-Level Pathway

Requirements:

- 30–36 graduate credits
- 10–12 advanced courses
- 120–144 advanced micro-credentials

Purpose:

- demonstrate advanced disciplinary or interdisciplinary mastery
- complete graduate-level research
- produce applied systems or scholarly artifacts
- engage in advanced faculty-reviewed portfolio assessment

---

## Doctoral-Level Contribution

Doctoral work is based on original contribution to knowledge.

Evidence may include:

- dissertation or equivalent project
- deployed system
- public research repository
- institutional framework
- ontology or knowledge graph
- theological or philosophical system
- blockchain governance model
- AI system
- published body of work

Doctoral work must include:

- research question
- literature or field review
- original contribution
- methodology
- evidence archive
- faculty or committee review
- public defense or review process

---

## Pathway Principle

Certificates demonstrate focused competency.

Degrees demonstrate structured mastery.

Doctoral work demonstrates original contribution.
