# Micro-Credential Credit Map

## Core Mapping

| Micro-Credentials | Credit Hours | Academic Unit |
|---:|---:|---|
| 1 | 0.25 | Micro-Credential |
| 4 | 1 | Credit Cluster |
| 12 | 3 | Standard Course |
| 48 | 12 | Foundational Certificate |
| 120 | 30 | Professional Certificate |
| 240 | 60 | Associate-Level Pathway |
| 480 | 120 | Bachelor-Level Pathway |

---

## Micro-Credential Design

Each micro-credential should include:

- title
- code
- learning outcome
- competency category
- estimated hours
- required evidence
- assessment rubric
- mastery threshold
- faculty review
- portfolio location
- transcript mapping

---

## Credit Cluster

A credit cluster contains 4 micro-credentials.

4 micro-credentials = 1 credit hour

Credit clusters may be used for:

- modular transfer review
- continuing education mapping
- workforce alignment
- prior learning assessment
- portfolio evaluation

---

## Standard Course

A standard course contains 12 micro-credentials.

12 micro-credentials = 3 credit hours

Each course must include:

- course description
- course learning outcomes
- 12 mapped micro-credentials
- assessment plan
- final portfolio artifact
- Faculty-of-Record approval
