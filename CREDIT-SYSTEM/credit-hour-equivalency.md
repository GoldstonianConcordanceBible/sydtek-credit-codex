# Credit Hour Equivalency Framework

## Core Formula

SydTek uses the following internal academic structure:

1 micro-credential = 0.25 credit hours

4 micro-credentials = 1 credit hour

12 micro-credentials = 3 credit hours

1 standard course = 3 credit hours

---

## Student Engagement Hours

For accreditation-readiness mapping, SydTek defines:

1 credit hour = approximately 45 total student engagement hours

Therefore:

0.25 credit hours = approximately 11.25 total student engagement hours

3 credit hours = approximately 135 total student engagement hours

---

## Standard Course Model

Each standard SydTek course includes:

- 12 micro-credentials
- approximately 135 total student engagement hours
- defined learning outcomes
- required evidence artifacts
- faculty review
- portfolio mapping
- transcript logic

---

## Credit Recognition Rule

A micro-credential is not recognized for credit merely because a student completed an activity.

A micro-credential becomes credit-bearing only when:

- the learning outcome is defined
- the engagement hours are documented
- the evidence artifact is submitted
- the assessment rubric is applied
- the Faculty-of-Record approves completion
- the registrar records the result

---

## Institutional Principle

Credit is not only time.

Credit is time plus competency plus evidence plus faculty validation.
