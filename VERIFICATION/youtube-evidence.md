# YouTube Learning Evidence Framework

## Purpose

YouTube-based learning artifacts may function as evidence within SydTek’s competency-based education model.

Video-based evidence supports:

- communication competency
- reflection
- demonstration
- instruction
- presentation
- portfolio development
- public scholarship

---

## Acceptable Video Evidence

Examples include:

- presentations
- demonstrations
- tutorials
- reflections
- lectures
- project walkthroughs
- research summaries
- field documentation
- interdisciplinary analysis

---

## Review Requirements

Video uploads alone do not equal competency.

Faculty review must evaluate:

- clarity
- learning outcome alignment
- demonstrated understanding
- communication quality
- originality
- evidence of applied competency

---

## Documentation Requirements

Learners should document:

- video URL
- title
- competency demonstrated
- summary of content
- learning outcome alignment
- reflection statement

---

## YouTube Principle

Video-based learning creates visible explanation, communication, and demonstration of competency.
