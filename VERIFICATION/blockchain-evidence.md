# Blockchain Evidence Framework

## Purpose

Blockchain records may function as verifiable evidence within SydTek’s competency-based and public-verification framework.

Blockchain systems support:

- timestamped verification
- public auditability
- immutable transaction history
- governance participation
- credential verification
- decentralized record preservation

---

## Acceptable Blockchain Evidence

Examples include:

- wallet transactions
- smart contract interaction
- governance participation
- DAO voting
- NFT credential verification
- on-chain certificates
- treasury analysis
- decentralized exchange interaction
- blockchain analytics dashboards

---

## Review Requirements

Blockchain activity alone does not automatically equal academic competency.

Faculty review must evaluate:

- learning outcome alignment
- demonstrated understanding
- technical interpretation
- ethical use
- reflection quality
- evidence documentation

---

## Documentation Requirements

Learners should document:

- wallet or transaction reference
- platform used
- competency demonstrated
- explanation of activity
- associated artifact or reflection
- outcome alignment

---

## Blockchain Principle

Blockchain provides transparent verification of participation and interaction.

Academic competency requires interpretation, reflection, and assessment.
