# Portfolio Evidence Framework

## Purpose

The portfolio serves as the central evidence archive for SydTek learners.

The portfolio organizes artifacts across:

- courses
- micro-credentials
- certificates
- degree pathways
- interdisciplinary projects
- public scholarship
- professional development

---

## Portfolio Categories

Portfolios may include:

- written work
- research artifacts
- GitHub repositories
- blockchain records
- presentations
- reflections
- technical projects
- field documentation
- governance participation
- interdisciplinary systems

---

## Portfolio Standards

Portfolios should demonstrate:

- organization
- evidence quality
- outcome alignment
- reflection
- revision history
- interdisciplinary integration
- long-term accessibility

---

## Portfolio Review

Portfolios may be reviewed at:

- course completion
- certificate completion
- pathway milestones
- capstone review
- graduate review
- doctoral review

---

## Portfolio Principle

The portfolio is the living academic record of demonstrated competency.
