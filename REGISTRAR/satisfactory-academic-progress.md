# Satisfactory Academic Progress Policy

## Purpose

Satisfactory Academic Progress defines the minimum academic progress learners must maintain to remain in good standing.

---

## SAP Measures

SydTek may evaluate progress using:

- credits attempted
- credits completed
- micro-credentials completed
- mastery level achieved
- portfolio submission status
- pace of completion
- academic integrity standing
- faculty review status

---

## Minimum Standards

Recommended minimum standards:

- maintain required mastery level of Proficient or higher
- complete at least 67% of attempted competencies
- remain within maximum program completion timeframe
- resolve revision-required artifacts within stated deadlines
- maintain academic integrity compliance

---

## Academic Standing Categories

Possible categories:

- Good Standing
- Warning
- Probation
- Suspension
- Dismissal
- Reinstated

---

## Appeals

Students may appeal SAP decisions by submitting:

- written appeal
- explanation of circumstances
- academic improvement plan
- supporting documentation

---

## SAP Principle

Progress must be measurable, documented, fair, and consistently applied.
