# Academic Calendar Framework

## Purpose

The academic calendar defines the official instructional rhythm for SydTek’s competency-based learning model.

Because competency-based education may be flexible, SydTek may use term-based, subscription-based, cohort-based, or self-paced structures.

---

## Calendar Models

### Term-Based Model

Courses begin and end on defined dates.

Suitable for:

- traditional academic review
- cohort learning
- financial aid compliance
- accreditation reporting

### Subscription-Based Model

Learners progress within defined enrollment periods.

Suitable for:

- competency-based education
- asynchronous learning
- workforce training

### Cohort-Based Model

Students move through structured learning together.

Suitable for:

- certificate programs
- professional development
- ministry cohorts
- doctoral seminars

### Self-Paced Model

Students progress through competencies individually.

Suitable for:

- prior learning assessment
- micro-credential completion
- professional learners

---

## Required Calendar Fields

Each calendar should define:

- term name
- start date
- end date
- registration period
- drop/add deadline
- withdrawal deadline
- assessment deadline
- final portfolio review period
- transcript posting date

---

## Calendar Principle

Flexible learning still requires clear academic time boundaries.
