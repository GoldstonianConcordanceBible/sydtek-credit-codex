# Transfer Credit Policy

## Purpose

The Transfer Credit Policy defines how external academic work may be reviewed for possible application toward SydTek pathways.

Transfer credit is not automatic.

It requires institutional review.

---

## Eligible Sources

Transfer review may include:

- accredited colleges or universities
- recognized training providers
- professional certifications
- military training
- verified workplace learning
- prior academic coursework
- approved competency-based programs

---

## Review Criteria

Transfer requests should be reviewed for:

- institutional legitimacy
- course level
- credit value
- learning outcomes
- grade or mastery result
- recency
- discipline alignment
- evidence quality

---

## Transfer Limits

Each program should define:

- maximum transfer credits allowed
- minimum credits completed through SydTek
- required residency or institutional credit
- non-transferable capstone or final portfolio requirements

---

## Transfer Principle

Transfer credit is awarded for equivalent learning, not merely similar titles.
