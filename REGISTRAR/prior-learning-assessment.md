# Prior Learning Assessment Policy

## Purpose

Prior Learning Assessment allows learners to receive academic recognition for college-level learning gained outside traditional classrooms.

This may include professional, technical, ministry, military, entrepreneurial, research, or field-based experience.

---

## Eligible Evidence

Prior learning evidence may include:

- professional portfolio
- certifications
- work products
- publications
- GitHub repositories
- blockchain records
- ministry experience
- research projects
- employer verification
- military training
- public presentations
- technical systems
- field documentation

---

## PLA Review Process

1. Learner submits PLA request.
2. Learner maps evidence to course or program outcomes.
3. Faculty reviewer evaluates evidence.
4. Rubric is applied.
5. Credit recommendation is made.
6. Academic Council or registrar approves credit.
7. Approved credit is recorded.

---

## PLA Restrictions

PLA should not replace:

- required capstones
- doctoral defense
- final portfolio review
- institution-specific formation requirements
- required residency credits

---

## PLA Principle

Experience alone is not credit.

Documented, college-level learning demonstrated through evidence may be considered for credit.
