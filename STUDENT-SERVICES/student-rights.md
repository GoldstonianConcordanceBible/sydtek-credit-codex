# Student Rights and Responsibilities

## Purpose

This document defines the basic rights and responsibilities of learners participating in SydTek’s competency-based academic framework.

---

## Student Rights

Students have the right to:

- clear academic expectations
- published policies
- fair assessment
- access to grievance procedures
- reasonable accessibility accommodations
- privacy of official academic records
- consistent application of academic standards
- timely review of submitted work
- non-retaliation for good-faith complaints
- accurate transcript records

---

## Student Responsibilities

Students are responsible for:

- academic integrity
- timely submission of work
- accurate representation of evidence
- respectful participation
- maintaining access to required tools
- following catalog policies
- responding to faculty feedback
- preserving portfolio evidence
- meeting satisfactory academic progress standards

---

## Public Evidence Notice

Students may choose or be required to submit public evidence in some pathways.

The institution should clearly disclose when evidence is public, private, or institutionally restricted.

---

## Student Rights Principle

A transparent academic system must protect both public verification and student dignity.
