# Student Records Retention Policy

## Purpose

The Student Records Retention Policy defines how academic records, evidence archives, transcript data, assessment records, and institutional documentation are preserved.

---

## Records May Include

- application records
- enrollment records
- course completion records
- micro-credential records
- assessment rubrics
- faculty review notes
- transcript records
- portfolio evidence links
- grievance records
- accessibility records
- prior learning assessment records

---

## Retention Categories

| Record Type | Recommended Retention |
|---|---:|
| Transcript records | Permanent |
| Degree or certificate completion records | Permanent |
| Assessment records | 5–7 years |
| Grievance records | 5–7 years |
| Accessibility records | 5–7 years |
| Advising records | 3–5 years |
| Application records | 3–5 years |

---

## Data Protection

Student records should be protected through appropriate privacy, access control, and records management practices.

Public evidence links may exist outside institutional systems, but official academic records must be institutionally maintained.

---

## Records Principle

Public evidence supports transparency.

Institutional records preserve academic authority.
