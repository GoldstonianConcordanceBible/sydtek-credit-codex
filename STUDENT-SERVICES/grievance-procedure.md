# Student Grievance Procedure

## Purpose

The Student Grievance Procedure provides a fair process for learners to raise concerns related to academic decisions, policies, services, conduct, accessibility, or institutional treatment.

---

## Grievable Issues

A grievance may involve:

- academic evaluation concern
- faculty review dispute
- accessibility concern
- advising issue
- policy interpretation
- student services concern
- discrimination concern
- records concern
- procedural fairness concern

---

## Grievance Process

1. Informal resolution attempt  
2. Written grievance submission  
3. Administrative review  
4. Evidence review  
5. Written response  
6. Appeal option, if applicable  
7. Final institutional decision  

---

## Written Grievance Should Include

- learner name or ID
- issue description
- date of issue
- policy or decision being challenged
- evidence or documentation
- requested resolution

---

## Non-Retaliation

Learners may file grievances without retaliation.

Retaliation for filing a grievance, participating in review, or requesting support is prohibited.

---

## Grievance Principle

A credible institution must provide students with a clear, fair, documented path for raising concerns.
