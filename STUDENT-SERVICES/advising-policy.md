# Academic Advising Policy

## Purpose

Academic advising supports learners as they navigate SydTek’s competency-based education framework, micro-credential pathways, portfolio requirements, and academic progress expectations.

Advising exists to help learners understand:

- program requirements
- micro-credential sequencing
- credit expectations
- portfolio development
- satisfactory academic progress
- transfer or prior learning options
- academic support resources

---

## Advising Functions

Academic advising may include:

- pathway planning
- course selection support
- portfolio planning
- progress monitoring
- referral to support services
- clarification of academic policies
- review of completion timelines

---

## Learner Responsibility

Learners are responsible for:

- reviewing catalog requirements
- tracking progress
- submitting required evidence
- meeting deadlines
- requesting support when needed
- maintaining academic integrity

---

## Advising Principle

Advising supports student success but does not replace learner responsibility.
