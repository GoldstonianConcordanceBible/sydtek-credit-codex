# Accessibility Policy

## Purpose

SydTek University is committed to providing reasonable access to learners with documented accessibility needs.

This policy supports equitable participation in competency-based, online, public-verification, and portfolio-based learning environments.

---

## Accessibility Areas

Accessibility considerations may include:

- course materials
- videos
- captions
- documents
- digital platforms
- assessments
- portfolio submissions
- synchronous meetings
- technical tools

---

## Accommodation Process

Learners requesting accommodation should submit:

- accommodation request
- description of need
- supporting documentation, when required
- requested academic adjustment

The institution should review requests through a documented process.

---

## Reasonable Accommodations May Include

- extended deadlines
- alternative format materials
- captioned media
- accessible documents
- modified assessment format
- assistive technology compatibility
- alternative evidence submission format

---

## Accessibility Principle

Accessibility strengthens academic fairness while maintaining learning outcomes and competency standards.
