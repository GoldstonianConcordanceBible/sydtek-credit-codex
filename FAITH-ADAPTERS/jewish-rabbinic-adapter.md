# Jewish / Rabbinic Faith Adapter

## Purpose

The Jewish Rabbinic Faith Adapter supports institutions integrating Torah, Talmudic reasoning, halakhic jurisprudence, and covenantal formation grounded in classical Rabbinic tradition — distinct from Messianic Judaism in its interpretation of covenantal identity and textual authority.

-----

## Optional Components

A Rabbinic Jewish implementation may include:

- Torah (Written Law) as primary revelatory foundation
- Talmud (Oral Law) — Mishnah and Gemara
- Halakha (Jewish legal reasoning and practice)
- Tanakh as complete Hebrew scriptural canon
- Midrash and Aggadah (narrative and homiletic tradition)
- Jewish ethics (Mussar movement, Tikun Olam, Tzedakah)
- Major commentators: Rashi, Maimonides (Rambam), Nachmanides (Ramban)
- Jewish liturgical calendar and cycle (Shabbat, Yamim Tovim)
- Kabbalah and Jewish mysticism (optional/advanced)
- Jewish denominational distinctions (Orthodox, Conservative, Reform, Reconstructionist)

-----

## Formation Outcomes

Learners may demonstrate:

- Torah and Talmud literacy at an applied level
- comprehension of halakhic reasoning as a jurisprudential framework
- ethical formation grounded in Mussar and covenantal obligation
- awareness of Jewish denominational diversity and interpretive traditions
- integration of Jewish calendar and communal rhythm into institutional formation
- applied understanding of Tikun Olam in civic and governance contexts
- textual reasoning skills developed through Chevruta and Beit Midrash models

-----

## Adapter Principle

The Rabbinic Jewish adapter supports Torah-centered and halakhic formation while remaining mapped to the shared CBE infrastructure.

-----

## Notes for Implementation

- Differentiation across Orthodox, Conservative, Reform, and Reconstructionist denominations is available at the institutional level.
- Hebrew literacy components may be integrated at the discretion of the implementing institution.
- Comparative modules with the Messianic Adapter may explore shared textual foundations with distinct interpretive outcomes.
