# Hindu Faith Adapter

## Purpose

The Hindu Faith Adapter supports institutions integrating Vedic scripture, dharmic philosophy, and liberation-centered formation across the diverse traditions of Sanatana Dharma.

-----

## Optional Components

A Hindu implementation may include:

- Vedas and Upanishads as primary scriptural foundations
- Bhagavad Gita as ethical and philosophical formation text
- Dharma, Karma, Moksha, and Samsara as doctrinal architecture
- Four Paths of Yoga: Jnana (knowledge), Bhakti (devotion), Karma (action), Raja (meditation)
- Atman and Brahman — individual and universal consciousness
- Puranas and Itihasas (Ramayana, Mahabharata)
- Six Schools of Hindu Philosophy (Darshanas)
- Puja, ritual practice, and sacred calendar
- Hindu ethics (Yamas, Niyamas, Ahimsa, Satya)
- Vedanta, Shaivism, Vaishnavism, and Shaktism traditions

-----

## Formation Outcomes

Learners may demonstrate:

- foundational literacy in Vedic and Upanishadic philosophy
- applied understanding of dharmic ethical frameworks
- comprehension of Atman-Brahman doctrine and its institutional implications
- integration of yogic paths into personal and professional formation
- awareness of diverse Hindu philosophical schools and their distinctions
- cosmological and systems thinking drawn from Hindu metaphysics
- civic formation grounded in dharmic values

-----

## Adapter Principle

The Hindu adapter supports dharma-centered and Vedic formation while remaining mapped to the shared CBE infrastructure.

-----

## Notes for Implementation

- Content may be differentiated across Advaita, Dvaita, Vishishtadvaita, and other Vedantic schools.
- Sanskrit literacy components may be integrated at the discretion of the implementing institution.
- Interfaith comparative modules may draw on shared Dharmic lineage with Buddhist and Jain traditions.
