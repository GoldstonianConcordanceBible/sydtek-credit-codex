# Orthodox Faith Adapter

## Purpose

The Orthodox Faith Adapter supports institutions aligned with Eastern Orthodox, Oriental Orthodox, or related sacramental and liturgical traditions.

---

## Optional Components

An Orthodox implementation may include:

- liturgical formation
- patristic theology
- sacramental worldview
- iconographic tradition
- conciliar theology
- spiritual discipline
- ecclesial authority
- ancient Christian continuity

---

## Formation Outcomes

Learners may demonstrate:

- understanding of Orthodox theology
- awareness of liturgical tradition
- engagement with patristic sources
- ethical and spiritual formation
- respect for ecclesial tradition
- integration of worship and learning

---

## Faculty Alignment

Faculty may be expected to respect or affirm institutional Orthodox identity depending on the implementation.

---

## Adapter Principle

The Orthodox adapter adds liturgical, sacramental, and ancient Christian formation while preserving competency-based academic structure.
