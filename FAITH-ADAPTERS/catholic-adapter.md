# Catholic Faith Adapter

## Purpose

The Catholic Faith Adapter supports institutions aligned with Catholic intellectual tradition, sacramental theology, moral teaching, social doctrine, and ecclesial formation.

---

## Optional Components

A Catholic implementation may include:

- Catholic social teaching
- sacramental theology
- moral theology
- church history
- magisterial tradition
- liturgical formation
- service learning
- human dignity framework

---

## Formation Outcomes

Learners may demonstrate:

- understanding of Catholic intellectual tradition
- ethical reasoning through Catholic social teaching
- awareness of sacramental theology
- service orientation
- respect for church tradition
- integration of faith and reason

---

## Adapter Principle

The Catholic adapter adds ecclesial, sacramental, moral, and social teaching formation to the academic framework.
