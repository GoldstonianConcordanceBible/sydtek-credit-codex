# Shamanism / Animism Adapter

## Purpose

The Shamanism and Animism Adapter supports institutions integrating spirit-world navigation, soul ecology, and animist cosmology as formation frameworks — drawing from the cross-cultural shamanic tradition as humanity’s oldest recorded spiritual technology.

-----

## The Distinction from Indigenous Adapter

The Indigenous/Traditional Adapter is community-specific and nation-anchored.
The Shamanism/Animism Adapter addresses the **cross-cultural underlying framework** that appears independently across Siberian, Mongolian, Korean (Musok), Andean (Curanderismo), West African, Native American, Celtic, and Arctic traditions — the recurring human discovery that:

- the world is alive and relational
- some humans can navigate between visible and invisible worlds
- healing, governance, and wisdom require consultation with more than the human layer of reality

-----

## Optional Components

A Shamanism / Animism implementation may include:

- animism — the living intelligence of all things (stones, animals, rivers, ancestors)
- the Three Worlds cosmology: upper, middle, lower world
- the shaman/curandero/medicine person as institution navigator and healer
- soul retrieval and soul loss as a psychology and formation framework
- spirit allies, power animals, and relational cosmology
- plant medicine traditions and their spiritual frameworks (Ayahuasca, Peyote, Psilocybin contexts)
- drumming, trance, and altered state access as formation technology
- death and the afterlife in animist cosmology
- ecological reciprocity — humans as one layer in a multi-species commons
- neo-shamanism (Michael Harner, core shamanism) as a reconstructed cross-cultural framework
- healing as community restoration, not only individual cure

-----

## Formation Outcomes

Learners may demonstrate:

- foundational literacy in animist cosmology and shamanic tradition
- applied understanding of three-worlds framework as a systems and navigation model
- comprehension of soul ecology as a psychological and institutional wellness framework
- integration of ecological reciprocity into governance and institutional design
- awareness of shamanic traditions across cultures and their structural similarities
- relational cosmology applied to community formation and conflict resolution
- capacity for cross-cultural spiritual dialogue grounded in animist epistemology

-----

## Adapter Principle

The Shamanism and Animism adapter supports spirit-relational, ecologically reciprocal, and soul-ecology-centered formation while remaining mapped to the shared CBE infrastructure.

-----

## Notes for Implementation

- This adapter addresses structural cross-cultural shamanism, not any single nation’s specific tradition — those belong to the Indigenous/Traditional Adapter.
- Plant medicine and altered state components require significant legal, safety, and cultural protocol documentation.
- Three-worlds cosmology is directly applicable to systems thinking, layered architecture, and multi-stakeholder navigation frameworks.
- Soul retrieval as a metaphor framework (restoring fragmented institutional identity) is deployable in organizational healing contexts.
