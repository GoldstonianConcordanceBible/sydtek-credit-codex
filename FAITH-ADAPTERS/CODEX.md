## What This Is

The Faith Adapter Codex is the official system for personalized learning companions inside the SydTek Credit Codex.

Each Faith Adapter is an acceptable assistant, not a replacement for a teacher, pastor, parent, professor, or institution.

Adapters help learners understand books, courses, certificates, and transfer-credit pathways through the lens of their background, tradition, tone, learning style, and level of readiness.

The current Faith Adapter registry contains 39 adapters across Abrahamic, Dharmic, East Asian, African, Indigenous, contemplative, pluralist, esoteric, and belief-threshold categories. ## Official Definition

A Faith Adapter is a personalized assistant that helps a learner enter the SydTek University and GCB ecosystem through a familiar worldview, communication style, and learning pathway.

It does not replace doctrine.

It adapts presentation.

It does not change truth.

It changes access.

## The Adapter Principle

Truth does not change, but the path of understanding must meet the learner where they are.
