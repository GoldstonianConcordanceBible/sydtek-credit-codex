# Secular / Atheist / Skeptic Adapter

## Purpose

The Secular Adapter supports institutions serving learners who operate from empirical, humanist, or skeptic frameworks — including atheists, agnostics, secular philosophers, and evidence-based practitioners who engage theological texts from a critical or academic standpoint.

-----

## Optional Components

A Secular implementation may include:

- empirical epistemology
- scientific methodology and philosophy of science
- secular ethics and moral philosophy
- critical theory and textual analysis
- comparative religion as academic discipline
- existential and phenomenological philosophy
- humanist formation frameworks
- evidence-based reasoning structures

-----

## Formation Outcomes

Learners may demonstrate:

- rigorous application of empirical reasoning
- critical literacy across religious and philosophical texts
- secular ethical formation without doctrinal dependency
- ability to engage theological arguments analytically
- grounding in philosophical ethics (Kantian, utilitarian, virtue-based)
- systems thinking applied to meaning, community, and governance
- intellectual humility and epistemic accountability

-----

## Adapter Principle

The Secular adapter supports evidence-based formation and critical inquiry while remaining mapped to the shared CBE infrastructure.

-----

## Notes for Implementation

- Theological content within the shared CBE infrastructure may be engaged as historical, philosophical, or literary artifact.
- Formation outcomes are grounded in verifiable reasoning and ethical coherence rather than doctrinal assent.
- Compatible with academic theology programs, philosophy of religion coursework, and interdisciplinary research tracks.
