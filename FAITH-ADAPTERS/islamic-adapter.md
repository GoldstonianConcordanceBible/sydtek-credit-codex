# Islamic Faith Adapter

## Purpose

The Islamic Faith Adapter supports institutions integrating Quranic revelation, Prophetic tradition, Islamic jurisprudence, and Tawhid-centered formation across educational and governance frameworks.

-----

## Optional Components

An Islamic implementation may include:

- Quran as primary revealed text
- Hadith and Sunnah (Prophetic tradition)
- Tawhid (divine unity as doctrinal foundation)
- Five Pillars of Islam
- Islamic jurisprudence (Fiqh) and legal schools (Madhabs)
- Aqeedah (creedal theology)
- Seerah (Prophetic biography)
- Ummah formation and community governance
- Islamic ethics (Akhlaq)
- Classical Islamic scholarship (Kalam, Tasawwuf, Usul al-Fiqh)

-----

## Formation Outcomes

Learners may demonstrate:

- Quranic literacy and recitation foundations
- understanding of Tawhid as theological and governance anchor
- application of Islamic ethical frameworks in community and institutional settings
- comprehension of Prophetic tradition and its jurisprudential implications
- awareness of classical scholarly lineage and Madhab distinctions
- integration of faith and practice (Deen wa Dunya)
- civic and institutional reasoning grounded in Islamic values

-----

## Adapter Principle

The Islamic adapter supports Tawhid-centered and Sunnah-aligned formation while remaining mapped to the shared CBE infrastructure.

-----

## Notes for Implementation

- Content may be differentiated across Sunni, Shia, Ibadi, and Sufi traditions as relevant to the institution.
- Interfaith comparative modules may draw on shared Abrahamic lineage.
- Arabic literacy components may be integrated at the discretion of the implementing institution.
