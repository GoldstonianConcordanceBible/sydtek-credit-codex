# Scientology Faith Adapter

## Purpose

The Scientology Faith Adapter supports institutions integrating the applied religious philosophy of L. Ron Hubbard, Dianetics, and the doctrinal frameworks of the Church of Scientology into educational and formation systems.

-----

## Optional Components

A Scientology implementation may include:

- Dianetics as foundational text and applied spiritual technology
- L. Ron Hubbard writings as primary doctrinal source
- Thetan — spiritual nature and immortality of the individual
- The Bridge to Total Freedom as progressive formation structure
- Auditing practice and its role in spiritual development
- Eight Dynamics framework (individual to infinity)
- Study Technology (Study Tech) as instructional methodology
- Ethics and justice frameworks within Scientology doctrine
- Tone Scale and applied human behavior models
- Administrative Technology (Admin Tech) for organizational formation

-----

## Formation Outcomes

Learners may demonstrate:

- foundational literacy in Scientology doctrinal frameworks
- applied understanding of the Eight Dynamics as a systems model
- comprehension of the Bridge to Total Freedom as a progressive formation pathway
- integration of Study Tech principles into learning methodology
- ethical formation grounded in Scientology’s organizational ethics codes
- awareness of auditing as a structured introspective discipline
- applied understanding of Tone Scale in interpersonal and institutional contexts

-----

## Adapter Principle

The Scientology adapter supports applied religious philosophy and Hubbard-centered formation while remaining mapped to the shared CBE infrastructure.

-----

## Notes for Implementation

- Formation components align with the progressive structure of the Bridge to Total Freedom.
- Study Technology components may be deployed as standalone instructional methodology modules.
- Organizational formation tracks may cross-reference Admin Tech frameworks.
