# Bahá’í Faith Adapter

## Purpose

The Bahá’í Faith Adapter supports institutions integrating the writings of Bahá’u’lláh, progressive revelation, world unity doctrine, and the Bahá’í administrative order into educational and governance frameworks.

-----

## Optional Components

A Bahá’í implementation may include:

- Bahá’u’lláh’s writings as primary revelatory authority
- Progressive revelation as doctrinal framework
- Writings of the Báb and ‘Abdu’l-Bahá
- Universal House of Justice and Bahá’í administrative order
- Three Onenesses: God, religion, and humanity
- Bahá’í ethical principles: unity, justice, gender equality, elimination of prejudice
- Ruhi Institute materials and community formation curriculum
- Prayer, fasting, and pilgrimage as formation disciplines
- Bahá’í approach to science and religion harmony
- World peace and global governance theology

-----

## Formation Outcomes

Learners may demonstrate:

- literacy in Bahá’í scripture and core doctrinal frameworks
- applied understanding of progressive revelation as a theological model
- comprehension of the Bahá’í administrative order as a governance prototype
- ethical formation grounded in unity, justice, and service principles
- integration of Bahá’í perspectives on science-religion harmony
- civic formation oriented toward global justice and institutional transformation
- awareness of the Bahá’í community consultation model for collective decision-making

-----

## Adapter Principle

The Bahá’í adapter supports progressive revelation-centered and unity-oriented formation while remaining mapped to the shared CBE infrastructure.

-----

## Notes for Implementation

- Consultation process models may be cross-referenced with DAO governance frameworks.
- Science-religion harmony modules are compatible with secular and academic tracks.
- Interfaith comparative modules may draw on the Bahá’í framework of Abrahamic and world religious continuity.
