# Deist Faith Adapter

## Purpose

The Deist Adapter supports institutions integrating classical and modern Deism — the conviction that reason and nature reveal a Creator God who does not intervene in human affairs — as a formation framework that bridges theological belief and rational philosophy.

-----

## Optional Components

A Deist implementation may include:

- natural theology — the universe as evidence of a Creator without revealed religion
- reason as the primary faculty for understanding ultimate reality
- classical Deism: Voltaire, Thomas Paine (*The Age of Reason*), Thomas Jefferson
- Founding Father theology — Deist underpinnings of American civic philosophy
- the Watchmaker God argument (Paley, Newton) and its philosophical legacy
- rejection of revelation, miracle, and clerical authority
- universal natural law as ethical foundation
- Thomas Jefferson’s redacted Bible as a Deist formation text
- modern Deism and its relationship to pantheism and spiritual naturalism
- science and design — intelligent design arguments in Deist context
- morality grounded in natural law without supernatural sanction

-----

## Formation Outcomes

Learners may demonstrate:

- foundational literacy in Deist philosophical tradition
- applied understanding of natural theology as a reasoning framework
- comprehension of rational morality derived from natural law
- awareness of Deism’s role in Enlightenment and democratic governance philosophy
- integration of reason-centered ethics into institutional design
- capacity for theological engagement that does not require revealed scripture
- civic formation grounded in universal natural law principles

-----

## Adapter Principle

The Deist adapter supports reason-centered, natural law-anchored, and Creator-acknowledging formation while remaining mapped to the shared CBE infrastructure.

-----

## Notes for Implementation

- Deism is historically significant in civic and governance philosophy — Founding Father Deism modules are directly applicable to American governance formation.
- Natural law ethics modules are compatible with Catholic Natural Law and Secular Ethics adapter content.
- Modern Deism’s overlap with pantheism and spiritual naturalism may be explored in cross-adapter modules with Earth-Based and Contemplative adapters.
