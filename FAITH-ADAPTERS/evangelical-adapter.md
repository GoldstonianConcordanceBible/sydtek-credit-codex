# Evangelical Faith Adapter

## Purpose

The Evangelical Faith Adapter supports institutions rooted in evangelical Christian theology, biblical authority, discipleship, mission, and personal faith formation.

---

## Optional Components

An Evangelical implementation may include:

- biblical authority
- personal discipleship
- evangelism
- missions
- Scripture memorization
- apologetics
- Christian worldview
- ministry preparation

---

## Formation Outcomes

Learners may demonstrate:

- biblical literacy
- Christian witness
- discipleship practice
- ministry readiness
- apologetics competency
- ethical conduct
- service orientation

---

## Adapter Principle

The Evangelical adapter adds discipleship, mission, and biblical authority emphasis to the CBE foundation.
