# Christian Faith Adapter

## Purpose

The Christian Faith Adapter provides a broad framework for Christian institutions using the SydTek Credit Codex.

---

## Optional Christian Components

A Christian implementation may include:

- statement of faith
- biblical worldview integration
- spiritual formation outcomes
- Christian ethics
- ministry preparation
- discipleship framework
- Scripture-based reflection
- service learning
- chapel or worship participation

---

## Faculty Alignment

Faculty may be asked to:

- affirm institutional mission
- support Christian worldview integration
- model ethical conduct
- participate in spiritual formation goals

---

## Student Formation

Student formation may include:

- biblical literacy
- Christian ethics
- service
- discipleship
- worship
- vocation
- community responsibility

---

## Adapter Principle

The Christian adapter adds formation without weakening academic quality.
