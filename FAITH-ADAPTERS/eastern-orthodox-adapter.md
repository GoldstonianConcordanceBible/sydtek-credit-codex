# Eastern Orthodox Faith Adapter

## Purpose

The Eastern Orthodox Faith Adapter supports institutions integrating Holy Tradition, Patristic theology, Theosis as the formation goal, and the liturgical and conciliar life of the Orthodox Church into educational and governance frameworks.

-----

## Optional Components

An Eastern Orthodox implementation may include:

- Holy Scripture within Holy Tradition as co-equal revelatory framework
- Patristic writings (Church Fathers: Athanasius, Basil, Chrysostom, Gregory Palamas)
- Theosis (Deification) as the doctrinal telos of human formation
- Seven Ecumenical Councils as doctrinal and governance reference
- Divine Liturgy and liturgical calendar as formational structure
- Hesychasm and contemplative prayer (Jesus Prayer, stillness)
- Orthodox anthropology: image and likeness (Imago Dei), nous, soul
- Sacramental theology: Eucharist, Baptism, Chrismation, Confession
- Orthodox ecclesiology: conciliarity, autocephalous church structures
- Iconographic theology and the role of sacred art in formation

-----

## Formation Outcomes

Learners may demonstrate:

- foundational literacy in Patristic theology and Holy Tradition
- comprehension of Theosis as a formation framework applicable to institutional design
- applied understanding of conciliar governance and its organizational implications
- liturgical reasoning integrated into institutional rhythm and calendar
- contemplative formation grounded in hesychast practice
- Orthodox anthropological framework applied to human development models
- awareness of Eastern vs. Western theological distinctions

-----

## Adapter Principle

The Eastern Orthodox adapter supports Tradition-centered and Theosis-oriented formation while remaining mapped to the shared CBE infrastructure.

-----

## Notes for Implementation

- Differentiation across Greek Orthodox, Russian Orthodox, Antiochian, Coptic, and Ethiopian Orthodox traditions is available at the institutional level.
- Iconographic and liturgical art components may be integrated as formation modules.
- Conciliar governance models may be cross-referenced with DAO and decentralized governance frameworks.
