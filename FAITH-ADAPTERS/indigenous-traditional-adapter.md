# Indigenous / Traditional Spirituality Adapter

## Purpose

The Indigenous and Traditional Spirituality Adapter supports institutions honoring oral tradition, land-based cosmology, ancestral wisdom, and communal formation frameworks drawn from Indigenous and traditional religious systems worldwide.

-----

## Optional Components

An Indigenous / Traditional implementation may include:

- oral tradition as primary knowledge transmission system
- land-based cosmology and sacred ecology
- ancestral relationship and intergenerational formation
- ceremonial and ritual practice as formational structure
- Indigenous ethics: reciprocity, balance, right relationship
- community governance and elder council models
- traditional ecological knowledge (TEK) integration
- Indigenous language preservation and revitalization
- healing and restorative practices within community
- cosmological narratives specific to the tradition of the implementing institution

-----

## Formation Outcomes

Learners may demonstrate:

- literacy in the oral and cosmological traditions of the represented community
- applied understanding of reciprocity and right relationship as ethical frameworks
- comprehension of elder council and consensus models as governance structures
- formation grounded in ancestral identity and intergenerational responsibility
- awareness of land-based knowledge systems as alternative epistemologies
- integration of ceremonial rhythm into institutional formation cycles
- civic formation oriented toward communal healing and restoration

-----

## Adapter Principle

The Indigenous adapter supports tradition-specific, community-centered, and land-anchored formation while remaining mapped to the shared CBE infrastructure.

-----

## Notes for Implementation

- This adapter is intentionally non-prescriptive to honor the diversity of Indigenous and traditional systems globally.
- Implementing institutions are responsible for sourcing content from authorized knowledge keepers within their community.
- Oral tradition components may require non-text-native documentation strategies (audio, video, living knowledge protocols).
- Governance models (elder council, consensus) may be cross-referenced with DAO and decentralized governance frameworks.
