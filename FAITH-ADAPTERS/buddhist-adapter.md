# Buddhist Faith Adapter

## Purpose

The Buddhist Faith Adapter supports institutions integrating dharmic philosophy, contemplative practice, and liberation-oriented formation across Theravada, Mahayana, and Vajrayana traditions.

-----

## Optional Components

A Buddhist implementation may include:

- Four Noble Truths as foundational doctrinal framework
- Noble Eightfold Path as ethical and practical formation structure
- Three Jewels: Buddha, Dharma, Sangha
- Pali Canon (Theravada) and/or Mahayana sutras
- Karma, interdependence (Pratītyasamutpāda), and impermanence (Anicca)
- Mindfulness and meditation practice (Sila, Samadhi, Prajna)
- Bodhisattva ideal and compassion ethics (Karuna, Metta)
- Buddhist philosophy of mind and consciousness
- Engaged Buddhism and community formation

-----

## Formation Outcomes

Learners may demonstrate:

- literacy in core Buddhist philosophical frameworks
- applied understanding of the Noble Eightfold Path in ethical decision-making
- contemplative practice foundations integrated into formation
- comprehension of karma and interdependence as systems frameworks
- compassion-centered leadership and community ethics
- awareness of major Buddhist traditions and their distinctions
- integration of Buddhist philosophy with governance and institutional thinking

-----

## Adapter Principle

The Buddhist adapter supports dharma-centered and contemplative formation while remaining mapped to the shared CBE infrastructure.

-----

## Notes for Implementation

- Content may be differentiated across Theravada, Zen, Tibetan, and Pure Land lineages.
- Meditation practice components may be integrated as standalone formation modules.
- Secular mindfulness derivatives may be cross-referenced with the Secular Adapter.
