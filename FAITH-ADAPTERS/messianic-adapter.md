# Messianic Faith Adapter

## Purpose

The Messianic Faith Adapter supports institutions integrating Hebrew roots, biblical covenant, Messiah-centered interpretation, and Judeo-Christian continuity.

---

## Optional Components

A Messianic implementation may include:

- Hebrew Bible foundations
- covenant theology
- Messiah-centered interpretation
- biblical festivals
- Torah literacy
- apostolic writings
- Hebrew roots context
- Jewish-Christian continuity

---

## Formation Outcomes

Learners may demonstrate:

- biblical covenant literacy
- understanding of Hebrew roots context
- Messiah-centered interpretation
- awareness of Jewish-Christian continuity
- ethical formation through Scripture
- integrated Old and New Testament reasoning

---

## Adapter Principle

The Messianic adapter supports covenantal and Messiah-centered formation while remaining mapped to the shared CBE infrastructure.
