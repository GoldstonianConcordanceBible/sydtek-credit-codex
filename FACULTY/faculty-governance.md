# Faculty Governance Framework

## Purpose

The Faculty Governance Framework establishes academic oversight and shared institutional responsibility within the SydTek competency-based education model.

Faculty governance supports:

- academic quality
- curriculum review
- assessment integrity
- policy review
- institutional continuity
- accreditation readiness

---

## Academic Governance Bodies

Recommended governance structure:

| Body | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| Academic Council | Academic oversight and standards |
| Curriculum Committee | Program and course approval |
| Assessment Committee | Rubric and outcomes review |
| Faculty Review Committee | Faculty evaluation and appointment |
| Appeals Committee | Academic appeals and disputes |

---

## Academic Council Responsibilities

The Academic Council should:

- approve academic programs
- review learning outcomes
- approve assessment standards
- review faculty qualifications
- oversee academic integrity
- review institutional effectiveness reports
- recommend policy revisions

---

## Governance Participation

Faculty governance participation may include:

- committee participation
- policy review
- assessment calibration
- curriculum development
- portfolio review
- interdisciplinary collaboration

---

## Governance Documentation

Governance records should include:

- meeting agendas
- attendance
- decisions
- voting records
- policy revisions
- action items

---

## Governance Principle

Competency-based education requires institutional accountability.

Faculty governance creates academic legitimacy and continuity beyond any individual contributor.
