# Faculty Credentialing Policy

## Purpose

This policy defines minimum faculty qualification standards for SydTek University’s competency-based framework.

The institution recognizes both academic credentials and demonstrated professional expertise.

---

## Undergraduate Instruction Standards

Faculty supporting undergraduate-level instruction should possess one or more of the following:

- master's degree in the teaching discipline or related field
- terminal degree in the teaching discipline
- demonstrated professional expertise with institutional approval
- documented scholarly, technical, or institutional contribution

---

## Graduate Instruction Standards

Faculty supporting graduate-level instruction should possess:

- doctoral degree in the teaching discipline or related field
- equivalent terminal professional qualification
- recognized scholarly contribution
- institutional approval by the Academic Council

---

## Competency-Based Education Recognition

Because SydTek uses a competency-based model, demonstrated expertise may supplement formal credentials when approved through institutional review.

Examples may include:

- published research
- deployed systems
- recognized technical leadership
- public scholarly contribution
- institutional architecture development
- blockchain infrastructure leadership
- AI systems development
- interdisciplinary expertise

---

## Faculty Approval Process

Faculty appointments should include:

- credential review
- portfolio review
- disciplinary alignment review
- Academic Council approval
- conflict-of-interest disclosure
- institutional policy acknowledgment

---

## Faculty Principle

Faculty authority is based on demonstrated competency, disciplinary alignment, ethical conduct, and institutional accountability.
