# Faculty-of-Record Policy

## Purpose

The Faculty-of-Record ensures that competency-based learning and public evidence are translated into academically recognized outcomes through qualified institutional review.

Public verification supports evidence.

Faculty review establishes academic authority.

---

## Faculty-of-Record Responsibilities

The Faculty-of-Record is responsible for:

- approving course learning outcomes
- validating assessment rubrics
- reviewing student evidence
- assigning mastery status
- approving portfolio submissions
- maintaining academic integrity
- documenting completion records
- coordinating with the registrar
- supporting inter-rater reliability practices

---

## Required Documentation

Faculty review should document:

- course code
- learner identifier
- micro-credential identifier
- submitted evidence
- rubric score
- mastery determination
- revision requirements
- approval status
- review date
- reviewer name

---

## Academic Authority Rule

No artifact becomes transcript-recognized academic credit until approved by the designated Faculty-of-Record.
