# SydTek Credit Codex

## Faith-Neutral CBE Foundation for Public Verification, Micro-Credentials, and Philosophy in Action

The **SydTek Credit Codex** is a competency-based education framework designed to organize micro-credentials, public evidence, faculty review, portfolio assessment, and interdisciplinary learning into accreditation-ready academic pathways.

This repository is the **foundation layer**.

It is intentionally **faith-neutral at the infrastructure level** so it can scale across multiple future implementations, including:

- Christian education
- Judeo-Christian education
- interdisciplinary studies
- workforce education
- decentralized science
- AI education
- blockchain education
- theological education
- institutional training

Faith traditions, denominational structures, and doctrinal requirements may be added later through modular adapters without changing the academic infrastructure.

---

## Core Rule

**1 micro-credential = 0.25 credit hours**

**12 micro-credentials = 1 standard 3-credit course**

**1 standard course = approximately 135 total student engagement hours**

This means one micro-credential represents approximately:

**11.25 total student engagement hours**

---

## Institutional Principle

Traditional education often says:

**Trust us.**

SydTek says:

**Verify the evidence.**

Students do not merely complete hidden assignments.

They demonstrate competency through visible, reviewable, and portable evidence.

---

## Evidence May Include

- GitHub repositories
- blockchain transactions
- YouTube learning artifacts
- public portfolios
- written reflections
- applied projects
- research artifacts
- field documentation
- DAO governance participation
- smart contract interaction
- published codices
- institutional contributions
- verifiable credentials

Public verification supports academic review.

It does not replace faculty assessment.
